# Pilot coaching brief

**Coverage:** three MPL ID S18 series across two verified broadcast days — 1036 (EVOS 2–0 RRQ), 1037 (NAVI 0–2 AE), 1107 (ONIC 0–2 TLID). This is **not** a full-season brief. Each point is a hypothesis for review, not a proven team trait.

## 1. EVOS 1036 game 1: early-snowball draft only becomes useful when the early map trade is converted

**What the evidence says**

- The caster framed EVOS’s top-three draft as an early-snowball identity with Paquito/Eudora/Melissa (`OBS-1036-DRAFT`).
- The database confirms the picks and a 16:51 win with 22,472 vs 1,252 tower damage (`OBS-1036-G1-DB`).
- The caster also described EVOS securing the Lord (`OBS-1036-LORD`).

**What it does not say**

- Same-core games were not automatic wins: EVOS lost 1055 game 2, 1066 game 2, 1077 game 1 and 1103 game 1 with Paquito/Eudora; 1036 game 2 took 24:40 (`OBS-1036-COUNTER`).

**Prep question:** when EVOS draft Paquito/Eudora, what is the actual first conversion checkpoint — first Turtle, first outer turret, or first Lord? Which opposing lane assignments force them to spend the early lead without structure damage?

**Drill idea:** review the first six minutes of 1036 game 1 and the four counterexample games at the same checkpoints (first Turtle, first outer turret, first Lord). Record who initiated and what was traded. Do not label a pick “respect ban” or “winning draft” from one game.

## 2. AE 1037 game 1: a late base defense and Lord denial, not wire-to-wire control

**What the evidence says**

- The caster called an AE play a “clutch 1v3” and later described NAVI’s large 3v1 advantage and failed defensive Lord (`OBS-1037-CLUTCH`, `OBS-1037-DEFENSE`).
- The reviewed frame at 21:08 shows AE defending base, AE 17–15, with NAVI about 1.9k gold ahead (`OBS-1037-VISUAL`).
- The database records AE +2,031 gold at the end, with less tower damage than NAVI (20,158 vs 33,132) (`OBS-1037-G1-DB`).

**What it does not say**

- The 1v3 is caster framing and only partly visible; it is not proof of the cause of the win.
- Close-gold winners with less tower damage are not unique to AE (1074 game 2, 1078 game 2; `OBS-1037-COUNTER`).
- The audit verdict is **partially supported**; see `AUD-1037-01`.

**Prep question:** when AE is defending base with a small deficit, what is their counter-initiation trigger, and how should the attacking team avoid overcommitting to the Lord?

**Drill idea:** review the full threat→defense→Lord sequence, not just the replay. Map AE’s frontline/backline spacing, who is on the wave, and whether NAVI had a safe disengage. Compare with one other close AE game before calling it a pattern.

## 3. TLID 1107 game 1: early rotations and Rapid Boots converted into a fast, structure-heavy win

**What the evidence says**

- The caster highlighted early rotations toward Aran to limit ONIC’s map control and TLID’s Rapid Boots/Turtle pressure (`OBS-1107-ROTATION`, `OBS-1107-TURTLE`).
- Reviewed frames show TLID 14.0K vs 13.1K at 5:08, 15.3K vs 13.6K at 5:27, and 40.6K vs 34.7K at 12:58 (`OBS-1107-VISUAL`).
- The database records a 14:47 win with 32,659 vs 1,902 tower damage; game 2 was 12:25 with 45,031 vs 6,534 tower damage (`OBS-1107-G1-DB`).

**What it does not say**

- Tower damage is an outcome; TLID also lost games with substantial tower damage and won some with less (`OBS-1107-COUNTER`).
- The “40 LORD KILL” overlay is a season milestone, not a current Lord; the 07:06:20 frame was a YouTube Premium promo and was rejected.
- This is one game, not a season-long TLID signature.

**Prep question:** which early rotation/Turtle setups consistently create outer-turret damage before six minutes, and how can opponents punish the Rapid Boots investment?

**Drill idea:** chart TLID’s first two rotations and the first outer-turret damage in 1107 game 1 vs one TLID loss. Track whether the lead came from a fight, a wave, or an objective trade. Do not infer communication or intent from hero positions.
