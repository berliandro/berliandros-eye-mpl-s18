# AE — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 36 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/ae.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

AE are the clean-sweep early-snowball team: 10–6, 23–13, 9 sweeps, 12 blowout wins, 15:41 average, and a 3–1 close-game record. When the first skirmish goes their way, the game usually ends quickly.

## What to copy

- **Early conversion.** 1055 is the best example: multiple won lanes, a "perfect turtle", a free Lord in a 4v5, and a snowball to roughly 10,000 gold (`TOBS-src-s18-w03d02-1055-g1-010`, `-012`, `-013`, `-015`).
- **Draft efficiency.** Paquito 9–0, Nolan 7–0, Hirara 9–2, Eudora 6–1, Obsidia 5–1, Gloo 6–2. These are genuine team-specific returns, not just league-average picks.
- **Blue-side pressure.** 14–5 blue versus 9–8 red. The blue number is the best non-TLID split in the league.

## How to attack them

- **Contest the first Turtle.** 1081 is the counterexample: AE took first blood, but DEWA answered with two kills from Hazel and won the Turtle fight (`TOBS-1081-G1-005`, `-006`, `-009`). The early start did not convert.
- **Force red.** 9–8 red is merely good, and the losses cluster against teams that can absorb the first wave: 0–2 ONIC (1043), 0–2 DEWA (1081), 0–2 TLID (1089).
- **Ban the clean enablers.** Paquito, Nolan and Hirara are the highest-return picks. Removing one forces AE onto a less efficient core; test whether Gloo/Eudora volume holds.

## Drills

- First-six-minute denial: opponent concedes first blood but wins the first Turtle and outer turret.
- Red-side draft block: no Paquito/Nolan, force AE to create a new win condition.
- 10,000-gold comeback defense: practise holding high ground, then counter-initiating.

## Limits

The 1055 description is caster commentary. The sample of close games is four. No objective counts or gold timelines exist, so "conversion" is a review label, not a measured rate.
