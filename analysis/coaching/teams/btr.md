# BTR — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 39 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/btr.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

BTR are a 10–6 team with a +3 game differential: early-snowball tempo, seven deciders, five 0–2 losses, and a 2–3 close-game record. The series record is better than the control.

## What to copy

- **Early tempo.** 1045 frames the win condition as Uranus, Selena and Nolan pressure in the first 4–8 minutes (`TOBS-1045-G1-004`); 1052 shows the Turtle-control version, with BTR taking the Turtle count 2–1 and then a free Lord (`TOBS-1052-G1-005`, `-006`).
- **Top-team wins.** BTR beat NAVI 2–0 twice (1052, 1106), so the structure works against a contender.
- **Efficient role picks.** Uranus 7–1, Marcel 6–1, Mathilda 5–2; these are the pieces that actually convert.

## How to attack them

- **The Selena trap.** Selena is BTR's signature volume pick: 13 picks at 30.8%, almost all Morenooo. Do not ban it reflexively; make BTR prove it can win with it, then punish the mid-lane tempo.
- **5v5.** 1087 has the caster saying BTR have not yet solved 5v5 teamfights (`TOBS-1087-G1-007`). Force front-to-back fights and avoid the early skirmish script.
- **Deny the first Turtle.** 1080 is the clearest loss: TLID force Moreno out, take Turtle and purple buff, and convert Turtle into Lord (`TOBS-1080-G1-006`, `-009`). TLID swept BTR twice (1070, 1080); copy that shape.

## Prep questions

1. What is BTR's conversion after the first Turtle — outer turret, mid tower, or Lord?
2. Who is the 5v5 engage, and what happens when it is blocked?
3. Can BTR win without Selena? The 13-pick sample says they have not tried much else.
4. Is the 10–6 record driven by the early script or by opponent mistakes?

## Drills

- 5v5 front-to-back: no early skirmish, force BTR to engage into a formed frontline.
- Mid-lane diversification: scrim block with Selena unavailable.
- First-Turtle denial: opponent takes the first two Turtles and forces BTR to play from behind.

## Limits

The two NAVI wins are a real counterexample to a "BTR are fragile" story. The Selena result is descriptive; a different matchup may change it. No objective counts or pick order exist.
