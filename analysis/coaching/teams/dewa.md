# DEWA — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 39 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/dewa.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

DEWA are an early-skirmish team with a collapse problem: 5–11, 14–25, 4–14 in blowouts, 7 series lost 0–2, and only one close game all season. They can win the first five minutes and still lose the map.

## What to copy

- **Early Turtle pressure.** 1062 is the model: an early Turtle fight, Hazel's double kill, and a prepared finish before minute 15 (`TOBS-1062-G1-003`, `-005`, `-007`). 1081 repeats it against AE: a teamfight turns into Turtle plus three kills (`TOBS-1081-G1-009`).
- **Ceiling games.** 2–0 AE (1081), 2–1 TLID (1085), and a 656-second game 1 over EVOS (1068) show the plan can beat good teams.
- **Aulus/Belerick/Claude volume.** The picks are not the problem by themselves; the conversion is.

## How to attack them

- **Survive the first five minutes.** DEWA's early leads are real but fragile. 1102 is the counterexample: they took the first two Turtles and lost the series (`TOBS-1102-G1-07`, `-08`, `-11`).
- **Force a structured mid-game.** 1039 was a 1–10 kill loss in 842 seconds (`TOBS-1039-G1-022`); 1062 was a one-sided win the other way. DEWA do not have a stable even-game plan.
- **Attack the draft volume.** No high-volume pick clears 50%: Aulus 45.5%, Belerick 36.4%, Claude 45.5%, Valentina 25%. Let them pick the volume and ban the conversion piece.

## Prep questions

1. What does DEWA do after the first Turtle — invade, rotate to a tower, or reset?
2. Who is the mid-game caller when the early lead stalls?
3. Why did the 1102 Turtle lead not convert? That clip is the priority review.
4. Is Hazel the trigger in every win, or is there a second carry?

## Drills

- Early-lead conversion: start at 6 minutes with a 3,000-gold lead and a Turtle; the objective is the first outer turret, not another kill.
- Blowout prevention: play from a 5,000-gold deficit and practise safe wave management.
- Draft simplification: reduce the pool to two win conditions and test them.

## Limits

Five wins include two against top-six teams, so this is not a free win. The 1102 counterexample is one series. No objective counts or gold timelines exist; "collapse" is a descriptive label from the scoreboard.
