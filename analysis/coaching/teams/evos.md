# EVOS — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 40 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/evos.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

EVOS are the league's .500 team: 8–8, 19–21, even side splits, 3–3 in close games, 7–7 in blowouts, eight deciders. They have two modes — a 16-minute early-tempo win and a 30-minute collapse — and the matchup decides which one appears.

## What to copy

- **The 1036 identity draft.** The caster calls the top-three an early-snowball draft; EVOS take all Lords and Turtles and win in 16:51 (`TOBS-1036-G1-002`, `-021`, `-028`). The draft is a real template, not a one-off pick.
- **Role flexibility.** 1094 shows Erlan on a fighter, Eudora zoning the Turtle, and a 965-second game 1 (`TOBS-1094-G1-002`, `-008`, `-012`).
- **Efficient secondary picks.** Obsidia 6–4, Carmilla 6–2, Cici 5–4. These are the pieces that hold the structure together.

## How to attack them

- **Stretch the game.** EVOS are even in close games and blowouts, but their worst loss (1055) is a 10,000-gold collapse after AE mirrors their mid movements (`TOBS-src-s18-w03d02-1055-g1-013`, `-015`). Force the late game and make them defend.
- **Deny the first Turtle.** 1036 is the clean win; 1055 is the clean loss. The first objective is the fork.
- **Draft pressure.** Paquito is 5–5 and Minotaur 2–5; Belerick 2–4. Ban Obsidia or Carmilla and force EVOS onto the weaker volume.

## Prep questions

1. Which EVOS team shows up when the first skirmish is even?
2. What is the 1036 draft's actual conversion checkpoint: first Turtle, first outer turret, or first Lord?
3. Why did the 1055 mid-game collapse happen — draft, rotation, or fight selection?
4. Can EVOS win a 30-minute game against a top-three team? The sample says not yet.

## Drills

- 10,000-gold deficit defense: hold high ground, trade space, find one counter-initiating fight.
- First-Turtle fork: two scrim blocks, one where EVOS take it, one where they lose it; compare the next 90 seconds.
- Late-game close: start at 20 minutes with an even gold line and practise the first pick.

## Limits

The .500 profile means any single matchup claim needs a specific opponent. The 1036 draft core also lost 1055 game 2, 1066 game 2, 1077 game 1 and 1103 game 1 (see the match report). No objective counts or gold timelines exist.
