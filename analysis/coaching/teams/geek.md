# GEEK — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 39 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/geek.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

GEEK are a 4–12 team with one elite matchup: two wins over ONIC, including 13–3 and 20–2 kill games. The rest of the season is a red-side and 5v5 problem: 7–17 red, seven 0–2 losses, 0–2 in close games.

## What to copy

- **The ONIC plan.** 1069: deny river control, play aggressively around Samuel and Kibo, take the first Turtle (`TOBS-1069-G1-005`, `-009`). 1076: the same plan produces the two most decisive kill lines of their season (`TOBS-1076-G1-012`).
- **Obsidia.** 8 picks at 62.5% is their best volume return.
- **Kimmy as a matchup tool.** 1084 explains the logic: GEEK picked Kimmy to answer BTR's Zhuxin, which struggles to reach her (`TOBS-1084-G1-006`).

## How to attack them

- **Ban Kimmy and force a standard 5v5.** Kimmy is a 10-pick comfort tool for Aboyy; without it, GEEK's draft is thinner. Their Paquito (7 picks, 14.3%) and Zhuxin (7, 28.6%) volume is losing.
- **Attack the red side.** 7–17 red is the second-worst split in the league. Force GEEK to red and make them draft a new plan.
- **Do not play the ONIC script.** GEEK's wins come from river chaos and early skirmishes. Slow the game, form a frontline, and make them engage into it.

## Prep questions

1. Why does the ONIC plan work and the same plan fail against TLID/AE/NAVI?
2. Who is the secondary carry when Kimmy is banned?
3. What is GEEK's first-Turtle conversion when the river fight is even?
4. Can GEEK win a game decided by under 3,000 gold? They are 0–2.

## Drills

- Red-side draft block: no Kimmy, no Paquito; force a new win condition.
- 5v5 front-to-back: GEEK must engage into a formed frontline, not a river skirmish.
- Close-game endgame: start at 18 minutes with a 1,500-gold lead.

## Limits

The ONIC matchup is a real strength, but it is one opponent. The 4–12 record and seven 0–2 losses are the stronger season signal. No objective counts or pick order exist.
