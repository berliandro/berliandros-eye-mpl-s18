# NAVI — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 37 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/navi.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

NAVI are the league's close-game team: 12–4, 25–12, 8 sweeps, five deciders, 4–3 in games decided by under 3,000 gold, and a 17:17 average. They play sustain-and-teamfight, absorb pressure, and win the messy game. They are less convincing when the opponent refuses to engage on their terms.

## What to copy

- **Close-game composure.** 4–3 in close games is the best record among contenders. The 1057 win over ONIC is the model: NAVI absorb ONIC's pressure and turn it back with a mature draft (`TOBS-1057-G1-010`).
- **Player production.** Zeonn (7.85 KDA) and Jiizee (7.54) are the top two qualified players by KDA. That is a stable base, not a hot streak.
- **Draft flexibility.** Hirara 14 picks at 64.3%, Belerick 10 at 70%, Paquito 10 at 60%, Cici 9 at 55.6%, Marcel 7 at 100%. NAVI can play through several cores.

## How to attack them

- **BTR is the blueprint.** BTR beat NAVI 2–0 twice (1052, 1106). 1052 is the explicit counterexample: the caster calls NAVI's draft a no-tank composition that is hard to engage, and BTR takes the Turtles and a free Lord (`TOBS-1052-G1-005` to `-008`). Deny the engage, force front-to-back, and make NAVI's frontline the problem.
- **Make the game fast.** NAVI's average is 17:17, the longest among the top three. A first-Turtle game with early structure damage compresses their preferred decision space.
- **Test the close-game record.** 4–3 is seven games. Do not treat it as clutch; force a close game with an early objective lead and see whether the pattern holds.

## Prep questions

1. Which NAVI player starts the engage, and who is the second layer? The 1047 caster frames the identity as sustain/teamfight; the film should identify the trigger.
2. What does NAVI do when Belerick or Hirara is banned? Their depth is the claim; test it.
3. Can NAVI win a game where they lose the first two Turtles? 1052 says no; find a counterexample or confirm.
4. Is the 4–3 close record driven by one player or by the draft?

## Drills

- Front-to-back disengage: force NAVI to engage into a tankless frontline and practise the reset.
- Fast-start scrim block: opponent takes the first Turtle, NAVI must respond within 90 seconds.
- Close-game endgame: start at 15 minutes with a 2,000-gold lead and practise closing.

## Limits

The BTR 2–0s are two series, not a law. NAVI also beat TLID 2–0 (1044, 1093) and EVOS 2–0 (1072). The transcript is commentary; no objective or gold-timeline data exists.
