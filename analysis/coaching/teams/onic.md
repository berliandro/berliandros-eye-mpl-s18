# ONIC — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 40 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/onic.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

ONIC are two teams: 15–6 on blue, 4–15 on red. The overall record is 7–9, 19–21, with eight deciders. The red-side gap is the largest single split in the league and the first thing every opponent should attack.

## What to copy

- **Blue-side draft and first selection.** 1091: the caster notes ONIC as home team has first selection with a much higher win rate (`TOBS-1091-G1-004`). The blue plan is real even if the cause is confounded.
- **Fast starts.** 1098: a 2,000-gold lead in the first four minutes, then four RRQ players killed into a Lord (`TOBS-1098-G1-007`, `-010`).
- **Turtle resilience.** 1102: ONIC lost the first two Turtles and still won the series 2–1 (`TOBS-1102-G1-08`, `-11`). That is the counterexample to a turtle-only game plan.

## How to attack them

- **Force red.** 4–15 is not a small sample. Prepare a red-side draft that denies Hirara/Nolan and forces Samuel mid to play a standard lane.
- **Punish the turtle trap.** 1076 was 3–13 and 2–20; 1062 was a one-sided loss. ONIC's early setups can be turned into counter-fights.
- **Pressure the mid experiment.** 1069 moved Samuel from roam to mid; test whether the new lane holds under pressure.

## Prep questions

1. What changes in ONIC's draft between blue and red? The answer is the highest-value review.
2. Who calls the first Turtle setup, and who is the sacrificial engage?
3. Can ONIC win a red-side game without first selection? Find one and copy it.
4. Is the 1102 comeback a repeatable adjustment or a DEWA collapse?

## Drills

- Red-side draft block: no first selection, two forced bans, build a new plan.
- Turtle defense: opponent starts the first Turtle; ONIC must trade for a tower or a pick instead of contesting into a loss.
- Mid-lane pressure: Samuel vs a standard mid, with jungle help delayed.

## Limits

Side is confounded with draft order and opponent. The blue record is not proof that blue causes wins. The 1102 comeback is one series. No pick-order or objective data exists.
