# RRQ — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 41 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/rrq.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`.

## Identity

RRQ are the league's decider team: nine series went to a third game, and they won four of 16. They are not getting blown out every week; they are losing close series. The draft has the clearest volume trap in the league: Paquito 17 picks at 23.5%.

## What to copy

- **1039 against DEWA.** RRQ survive the early DEWA push, take the Lord, and win 10–1 in kills (`TOBS-1039-G1-005`, `-012`, `-022`). That is the clean version of their plan.
- **1100 game 1 against NAVI.** First Turtle, 828-second finish, and a game-one win (`TOBS-1100-G1-07`, `-13`). The plan travels against a top team, even though the series was lost.
- **Joshua's pressure.** The 1065 caster says Joshua's early pressure and gold lead change the lane (`TOBS-1065-G1-007`); the 1091 caster warns that an unchecked Joshua becomes dangerous (`TOBS-1091-G1-006`).

## How to attack them

- **Let Paquito through, then punish it.** Paquito is 4–13 for RRQ. Forcing them onto the comfort pick may be better than banning it.
- **Make the first Turtle decisive.** 1065 loses both Turtles and the middle outer turret; 1087 takes the Turtle but loses four members (`TOBS-1065-G1-006` to `-009`, `TOBS-1087-G1-006`).
- **Close the series.** Nine deciders and a 1–6 sweep record say RRQ are beatable in game three. Prepare a decisive game-three draft, not a repeat of game one.

## Prep questions

1. Why does RRQ's early plan work in 1039/1100 and fail in 1065/1098?
2. Who is the secondary carry when Joshua is contained?
3. What is RRQ's game-three draft tendency? Nine deciders is enough to review.
4. Can RRQ win a game where they lose the first Turtle and the first outer turret?

## Drills

- Game-three block: start a scrim series 1–1 and practise the deciding draft.
- Paquito-volume test: let RRQ pick it, then deny the early conversion.
- Front-to-back defense: force RRQ to engage into a formed frontline instead of a pick.

## Limits

The 1039 and 1100 wins are real counterexamples to a "RRQ are weak" story. The Paquito rate is descriptive and includes different players. No objective counts or pick order exist.
