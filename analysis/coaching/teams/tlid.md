# TLID — coaching brief

**Evidence base:** 16 series, 37 games, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`. Season tables in `analysis/teams/tlid.md`; data appendix in `analysis/reports/league-data.md`. Transcript coverage is partial and commentary-based.

## Identity

TLID are the league's finisher: 12–4 series, 26–11 games, 9 sweeps, 15:14 average, 12 blowout wins and only one game decided by under 3,000 gold. They do not win by surviving chaos; they win by converting the first Turtle into structure damage before the opponent stabilises.

## What to copy

- **First-Turtle conversion.** 1107: Rapid Boots rotations contest the healing Turtle, a three-player zone locks ONIC's five in the top area, and the third Turtle opens the mid base turret (`TOBS-1107-G1-02`, `-05`, `-06`, `-07`). 1080: TLID force Moreno out, take Turtle and purple buff, then convert Turtle into Lord (`TOBS-1080-G1-006`, `-009`).
- **Draft depth.** Claude 16 picks at 68.8%, Paquito 14 at 71.4%, Hirara 9 at 88.9%, Zhuxin 8 at 87.5%, Belerick 9 at 77.8%. The team wins with several different cores, not one comfort draft.
- **Both sides.** Blue 15–3 and red 11–8. The blue number is inflated by draft/opponent context, but the red record is still good.

## How to attack them

- **Deny the first Turtle.** Every TLID loss is a candidate review: 0–2 NAVI (1044), 1–2 EVOS (1083), 1–2 DEWA (1085), 0–2 NAVI (1093). Start with 1083 and 1085 to see whether the opponent disrupted the first rotation or won the later 5v5.
- **Force a low-economy game.** TLID have one close game all season. Make the game messy, deny the clean Turtle-to-tower sequence, and test their late-game decision-making, which the database cannot describe.
- **Draft pressure.** Hirara and Zhuxin have the best returns; Claude and Paquito carry the volume. Ban one of the high-return picks and force TLID onto a lower-efficiency core.

## Prep questions

1. Who starts the first Turtle for TLID, and which three players form the zone?
2. What does TLID do when the first Turtle is contested and lost? 1083 and 1085 are the first clips.
3. Is Drichel or Keven the conversion trigger in the mid-game? Their KDA is similar (6.78 vs 6.51); the film should decide.
4. Can TLID win a game where they do not take the first Turtle?

## Drills

- First-five-minute rotation tracking: log every TLID rotation, the first Turtle contest, and the first tower damage.
- 3v3/3v5 zone escape: practise breaking the three-player lock without overcommitting.
- Red-side low-economy scrim block: no first Turtle, force mid-game.

## Limits

No objective counts, gold timelines or communications. The 1107 and 1080 descriptions are caster commentary, not measured events. The four losses are a small sample; do not build a rule from one.
