# Paquito — transcript notes

Hero page for Paquito. ASR spellings are preserved in the source quote; this page uses the canonical name where clear.

## Evidence-backed profile

**Season:** **87 picks (50% of games), 44.8% win rate, 58 bans, 83.3% contest.** Source: `Q-ALL-PICKS`, `Q-ALL-BANS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Read.** Paquito is the league's highest-volume pick and its clearest system trap. TLID (14 picks, 71.4%) and AE (9, 100%) win with it; RRQ (17, 23.5%), ONIC (7, 14.3%) and GEEK (7, 14.3%) lose with the same hero. The broadcast evidence explains the intended role — early-snowball punisher (`TOBS-1036-G1-002`), level-4 jungle threat (`TOBS-1039-G1-006`), fast rotation (`TOBS-1060-G1-008`) — but not why the team split is so extreme.

**Counterexample/limit.** The 44.8% season rate is the counterexample to "Paquito is a priority pick." It is a priority for specific teams with the structure to use it. Do not copy RRQ's volume.
