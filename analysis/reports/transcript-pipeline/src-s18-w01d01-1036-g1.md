## Limitations

- Only match 1036 game 1 was processed. Game 2 is out of scope for this prototype.
- The segment-type classifier is heuristic; accepted observations were manually
  corrected to the scene they actually describe.
- Raw transcripts have no speaker labels; `speaker` is `null` for every record.
- ASR variants (`Pakito`, `Yudora`, `Melisa`, `Albert`, `Randy`) are recorded as
  resolved unresolved-reference records, not silently normalized.
- Caster statements remain commentary, not proof of an in-game event.
- No visual verification was performed, and the visual-proof status is unchanged.
