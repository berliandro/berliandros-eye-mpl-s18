**Prep rules.**

1. Start from the opponent's volume picks, then the league ban-pressure list. Do not ban a hero just because the league does.
2. Do not first-pick a hero with a losing team split unless the player is the exception.
3. The database records lane order, not pick order. Any "first pick" claim needs transcript or video evidence.
4. Re-check the splits after any patch; the database has no patch field.
