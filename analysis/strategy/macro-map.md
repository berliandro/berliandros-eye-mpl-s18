## Evidence-backed analysis

**Side is a scouting question, not a law.** Blue won 101 of 174 games (58.0%). But side is confounded with draft order, opponent and map assignment. The team splits are far more extreme than the league average: ONIC 15–6 blue / 4–15 red, TLID 15–3 / 11–8, AE 14–5 / 9–8, RRQ 10–14 / 4–13, GEEK 6–9 / 7–17, DEWA 10–15 / 4–10. Force ONIC and RRQ to red if the draft process allows it; do not assume TLID or AE are vulnerable there.

**Maps.** 153 of 174 games have a recorded map; 21 do not. Sample sizes are small (12–38 games). Blue win rates range from 43.8% on Revealing Wisps (16 games) to 72.7% on Healing Turtle (22 games). Flying Cloud is the most played map (38) and the closest to even (52.6%). These are descriptive; no map is proven to favour a side.

**Duration.** League average 16:28, median 15:23, range 10:03 to 31:04. Golden Turret has the shortest average (14:37) and Dangerous Grass the longest (17:39), but team style and sample size confound both.

**What the transcript adds.** Map-specific mechanics and plans: Revealing Wisps vision (1087), healing Turtle targeting the strongest tower (1084), TLID's Rapid Boots rotations (1107), GEEK's river denial (1069). Treat these as review cues, not map win-rate explanations.

**Limits.** 21 games lack map mode. Pick order is not in the database. Map assignment may be controlled by the home team. Do not claim a map is "blue-sided" from 12 games, and do not compare map win rates across months as if a patch were constant.
