## Evidence-backed analysis

**The database cannot count objectives.** There is no Lord, Turtle, or tower-event table in the canonical snapshot. Any "average Turtles" or "Lord rate" number would be invented. Objective claims must stay transcript-attributed or become video-review checkpoints.

**What the transcripts repeatedly describe.**

- The first Turtle is the first conversion checkpoint: 1094 calls the meta "who gets the first turtle" (`TOBS-1094-G1-004`); 1080 describes TLID's 3–0 early Turtle control (`TOBS-1080-G1-002`); 1052 shows BTR taking the Turtle count 2–1 and then a free Lord (`TOBS-1052-G1-005`, `-006`); 1055 describes AE's "perfect turtle" and a free Lord in a 4v5 (`TOBS-src-s18-w03d02-1055-g1-010`, `-012`).
- Lord denial is a separate skill: 1036 describes EVOS denying RRQ information and getting a free Lord (`TOBS-1036-G1-015`); 1039 describes RRQ taking the Lord and denying DEWA any objective (`TOBS-1039-G1-012`); 1047 describes GEEK refusing to give NAVI a free Lord (`TOBS-1047-G1-010`).
- The conversion step matters more than the first objective. 1087 has RRQ taking the Turtle but losing four members (`TOBS-1087-G1-006`); 1102 has DEWA taking the first two Turtles and losing the series (`TOBS-1102-G1-07`, `-08`); 1057 has ONIC taking the first Lord and losing game one (`TOBS-1057-G1-007`).

**What can be measured.** Duration, final tower damage, kills and gold are in the database. Tower damage is the closest conversion proxy and is a team aggregate, not an objective count. Use these as outcomes, never as substitutes for objective events.

**Review protocol.** For each reviewed game, log the first Turtle winner, what was converted (tower damage or Lord), the first Lord winner, base damage, and whether the first-Turtle team won. That is the only honest way to build an objective dataset. Until then, publish sequences and questions, not averages.

**Coaching use.** Prepare the objective sequence: who starts the Turtle, who zones, what is traded, and what happens in the 60 seconds after. Do not prepare a "Turtle win rate" that the data cannot support.
