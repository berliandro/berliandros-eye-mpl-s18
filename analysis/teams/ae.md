## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 10–6 series, 23–13 games; 9 sweeps won and 3 lost; 4 deciders. Average game 15:41. Blue side 14–5, red 9–8. Close games (final gold difference under 3,000) 3–1; blowouts (10,000+) 12–6. Source: `Q-ALL-TEAM-SEASON`, `Q-ALL-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-TEAM-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-PICKS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Supported reading.**

- AE's wins are usually clean: 9 of 10 series wins were 2–0, and 12 of 23 game wins were blowouts. The 3–1 close-game record says they can also win a tight game, but the sample is small.
- The draft core is efficient. Hirara (11 picks, 81.8%), Paquito (9, 100%), Gloo (8, 75%), Eudora (7, 85.7%), Nolan (7, 100%) and Obsidia (6, 83.3%) all outperform their league baselines. This is descriptive: the players and team context are part of the number.
- The 1055 broadcast describes the intended mechanism: multiple won lanes, a "perfect turtle", a free Lord in a 4v5, and a snowball to roughly 10,000 gold (`TOBS-src-s18-w03d02-1055-g1-010`, `-012`, `-013`, `-015`). In 1074 the caster notes Paquito in the gold lane and Nolan in the jungle, with AE taking the first turtle (`TOBS-1074-G1-004`, `TOBS-1074-G1-008`).
- 1071 adds a different mode: the caster calls AE "fast but neat" and notes a shift to sustained teamfighting with Rafaela (`TOBS-1071-G1-006`, `TOBS-1071-G1-009`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- Six series were lost, including 0–2 to ONIC (1043), 0–2 to DEWA (1081) and 0–2 to TLID (1089). In 1081 AE took first blood but DEWA answered with two kills from Hazel and won the turtle fight (`TOBS-1081-G1-005`, `-006`, `-009`); the early-start story did not convert.
- Hero win rates are confounded by opponent and player; they are not evidence that a pick causes a win.
- Transcript coverage exists for only part of AE's season; the themes above are not a complete map of their drafts.

**Coaching read.** Force AE to win through a contested first Turtle rather than free lanes, and test their red-side plan (9–8) rather than their blue-side comfort (14–5). Paquito/Nolan are genuine priority picks for them, but banning one does not remove the structure.
