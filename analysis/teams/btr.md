## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 10–6 series, 21–18 games; 4 sweeps won and 5 lost; 7 deciders. Average game 16:50. Blue side 8–5, red 13–13. Close games (under 3,000 gold) 2–3; blowouts (10,000+) 5–6. Source: `Q-ALL-TEAM-SEASON`, `Q-ALL-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-TEAM-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-PICKS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Supported reading.**

- The 10–6 series record overstates control: BTR were +3 in games, lost five series 0–2, and went 2–3 in close games and 5–6 in blowouts. They are a playoff team with a narrow margin, not a dominant one.
- The clearest draft mismatch is Selena: 13 picks at 30.8%, almost all of them Morenooo's (13). That volume makes it a signature pick; the results make it a questionable one.
- BTR's healthier picks are structural: Uranus (8, 87.5%), Marcel (7, 85.7%), Mathilda (7, 71.4%) and Nolan (9, 55.6%). The 1045 broadcast frames the early-snowball condition around Uranus, Selena and Nolan (`TOBS-1045-G1-004`), and 1052 shows the turtle-control version of the plan (`TOBS-1052-G1-005`, `-008`).
- The 1080 loss to TLID is the clearest failure mode: TLID forces Moreno out, BTR loses the Turtle and purple buff, then TLID converts Turtle into Lord (`TOBS-1080-G1-006`, `-009`). BTR's own broadcast notes they had not yet solved 5v5 teamfights in 1087 (`TOBS-1087-G1-007`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- BTR beat NAVI twice (2–0 in 1052 and 1106), so the structure works against at least one top team. The same structure was swept twice by TLID (1070, 1080) and twice by AE (1067, 1092).
- Red side is neutral at 13–13; there is no side-based explanation for the gap between series record and game record.
- The Selena result is descriptive; it does not prove the pick is bad in every matchup.

**Coaching read.** Attack the mid-lane comfort pick and the first-Turtle setup, not the scoreline. Force BTR into 5v5 front-to-back fights and make Morenooo play a different tempo; test whether the early-snowball plan survives when Selena is unavailable.
