**Supported reading.**

- DEWA's season is a collapse profile: 7 series lost 0–2, 14 blowout losses against 4 blowout wins, and a 14–25 game record. The 1–1 close-game record shows they rarely reach a tight finish.
- No high-volume draft pick clears 50%: Aulus, Belerick and Claude were each picked 11 times at 45.5%, 36.4% and 45.5%; Valentina was 8 picks at 25%. The draft is volume without a reliable win condition.
- Their best broadcast evidence is the 1062 win over ONIC: an early turtle fight, Hazel's double kill, and a prepared finish before minute 15 (`TOBS-1062-G1-003`, `-005`, `-007`). 1081 repeats the pattern against AE: a teamfight turns into Turtle plus three kills (`TOBS-1081-G1-009`).
- 1068 game 1 shows the ceiling: a 656-second win over EVOS (`TOBS-1068-G1-012`), although DEWA lost the series.

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- DEWA beat TLID 2–1 (1085) and AE 2–0 (1081), so the ceiling is real. They are not a free win.
- 1102 is the counterexample to "win the early turtles": DEWA took the first two Turtles and still lost the series 1–2 (`TOBS-1102-G1-07`, `-08`, `-11`).
- 1039 shows the other failure: a 1–10 kill game and a 842-second loss to RRQ (`TOBS-1039-G1-022`).

**Coaching read.** The priority is not a new draft identity; it is converting the early leads they already create. Force DEWA to play from behind and make the first Turtle decisive, because their evidence says they do not recover from blowouts. Test whether the Aulus/Belerick/Claude volume can be narrowed to matchups.
