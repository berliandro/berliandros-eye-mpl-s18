## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 8–8 series, 19–21 games; 3 sweeps won and 5 lost; 8 deciders. Average game 17:11. Blue side 11–11, red 8–10. Close games (under 3,000 gold) 3–3; blowouts (10,000+) 7–7. **Supported reading.**

- EVOS are the league's .500 control group: even series, even games, even side splits, even close games, even blowouts, and eight deciders. Any claim that they are a strong or weak team needs a specific matchup.
- The draft is functional but not dominant: Obsidia (10, 60%), Paquito (10, 50%), Cici (9, 55.6%), Carmilla (8, 75%). Minotaur (7, 28.6%) and Belerick (6, 33.3%) are the weak-volume picks.
- 1036 is the clearest positive evidence: the caster calls the top-three an early-snowball "identity draft", EVOS take all Lords and Turtles, and the game ends in 16:51 (`TOBS-1036-G1-002`, `-021`, `-028`). 1094 shows the other mode: Erlan on a fighter, Eudora zoning the Turtle, and a 965-second game 1 (`TOBS-1094-G1-002`, `-008`, `-012`).
- 1055 is the failure case: AE mirrors the mid movements, EVOS fall to a 10,000-gold deficit, and the game becomes a high-ground defense (`TOBS-src-s18-w03d02-1055-g1-013`, `-015`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- The 1036 Paquito/Eudora core also lost 1055 game 2, 1066 game 2, 1077 game 1 and 1103 game 1; the draft is not automatic (see `analysis/matches/1036-evoss-vs-rrq.md`).
- EVOS lost 0–2 five times, including to TLID, AE, ONIC and NAVI; the .500 record hides a high-variance matchup profile.
- The transcript covers only part of the season; the "identity draft" language is caster framing, not team intent.

**Coaching read.** Prepare for two EVOS: the early-tempo team that wins in 16 minutes, and the team that gets stretched to 30 minutes. Deny the first Turtle and force them to defend; test whether their mid-game plan survives when the early skirmish is lost. Draft flexibility matters more than banning one core pick.
