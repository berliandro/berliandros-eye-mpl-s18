**Supported reading.**

- GEEK's season is two different teams: a 4–12 team overall, but 4 of those wins came against ONIC (twice), DEWA and EVOS. The 2–0 wins over ONIC in 1076 were 13–3 and 20–2 in kills (`TOBS-1076-G1-012`), which is the most decisive result in their season.
- Their red side is the problem area: 7–17 against 6–9 on blue. Only 2 of 13 games won were close, and they lost seven series 0–2.
- The usable draft pieces are Belerick (10, 40%), Kimmy (10, 40%), Obsidia (8, 62.5%), Alice (7, 42.9%) and Barats (7, 42.9%). Paquito (7, 14.3%) and Zhuxin (7, 28.6%) are the volume that did not work.
- 1069 describes the ONIC win: GEEK denies river control and plays aggressively around Samuel and Kibo (`TOBS-1069-G1-005`), taking the first Turtle (`TOBS-1069-G1-009`). 1084 shows the failure against BTR: BTR's counterattack removes three GEEK members after GEEK's kill (`TOBS-1084-G1-010`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- The ONIC matchup is a real strength; it is not evidence that GEEK can reproduce the plan against TLID, AE or NAVI.
- Kimmy's 40% win rate includes Aboyy's 10 picks; the hero is a player-specific tool, not a universal answer.
- Seven 0–2 losses mean the red-side draft problem is not a small sample.

**Coaching read.** Copy the ONIC plan, not the GEEK season: early river denial and a committed first Turtle. Against GEEK, ban the Kimmy comfort and force them to play a standard 5v5; their evidence says they lose those. The red-side plan is the first thing to fix.
