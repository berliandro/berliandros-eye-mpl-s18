# NAVI — NAVI transcript notes

Team page for NAVI (NAVI).

## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 12–4 series, 25–12 games; 8 sweeps won and 3 lost; 5 deciders. Average game 17:17. Blue side 12–5, red 13–7. Close games (under 3,000 gold) 4–3; blowouts (10,000+) 11–5. Source: `Q-ALL-TEAM-SEASON`, `Q-ALL-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-TEAM-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-PICKS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Supported reading.**

- NAVI are the league's best close-game team on this sample: 4–3 in games decided by under 3,000 gold, with five series going to a decider. That is a useful trait, but seven close games is a small base.
- The draft is sustain-and-teamfight first: Hirara (14, 64.3%), Belerick (10, 70%), Paquito (10, 60%), Cici (9, 55.6%), Claude (8, 62.5%), Valentina (8, 50%), Marcel (7, 100%). The 1047 caster explicitly frames NAVI's identity as sustain and teamfight (`TOBS-1047-G1-003`).
- The player base is a real strength: Zeonn (7.85 KDA) and Jiizee (7.54) are the top two qualified players by KDA in the league. That is production, not a draft story.
- 1065 shows the early version: Paquito and Lilia for early snowball, two Turtles without a trade, and a middle outer turret (`TOBS-1065-G1-004` to `-009`). 1057 shows the mature version: NAVI absorb ONIC's pressure and win game one (`TOBS-1057-G1-010`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- NAVI lost twice to BTR (0–2 in 1052 and 1106), and 1052 is the structural counterexample: BTR called NAVI's draft a no-tank composition that is hard to engage, then took the Turtles and a free Lord (`TOBS-1052-G1-005` to `-008`).
- 1091 is the other failure: ONIC kept Joshua unchecked and won the decider (`TOBS-1091-G1-006`).
- KDA and hero win rates are descriptive; they do not isolate player skill from team structure.

**Coaching read.** The BTR loss is the template: deny NAVI's engage and make them fight front-to-back without a tank. Force a fast first-Turtle game; NAVI's evidence is strongest in longer, close games. Do not assume their 4–3 close record is clutch — test it with a early objective lead.
