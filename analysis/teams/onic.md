## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 7–9 series, 19–21 games; 4 sweeps won and 4 lost; 8 deciders. Average game 17:10. **Blue side 15–6 (71.4%), red side 4–15 (21.1%).** Close games (under 3,000 gold) 2–4; blowouts (10,000+) 11–10. Source: `Q-ALL-TEAM-SEASON`, `Q-ALL-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-TEAM-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-PICKS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Supported reading.**

- The side split is the largest in the league and the first thing any opponent should test: ONIC won 15 of 21 blue-side games and only 4 of 19 red-side games. That is not proof of a causal side effect — side is confounded with draft order and opponent — but it is too large to ignore.
- The draft volume is not efficient: Hirara (10, 40%), Atlas (9, 44.4%), Belerick (9, 44.4%), Claude (8, 37.5%), Eudora (7, 42.9%), Paquito (7, 14.3%). Carmilla (6, 66.7%) and Nolan (7, 57.1%) are the exceptions.
- The 1091 win over NAVI is the best evidence of the blue-side plan: the caster notes ONIC as home team has first selection with a much higher win rate, and warns that an unchecked Joshua becomes dangerous (`TOBS-1091-G1-004`, `-006`). 1098 shows the fast-start version: a 2,000-gold lead in the first four minutes, then four RRQ players killed into a Lord (`TOBS-1098-G1-007`, `-010`).
- 1102 is the counterexample to a turtle-only story: ONIC lost the first two Turtles and still won the series 2–1 (`TOBS-1102-G1-08`, `-11`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- The red-side record includes 0–2 losses to NAVI (1057), AE (1074), GEEK (1076) and TLID (1107), plus 1–2 losses to BTR, TLID, DEWA, GEEK and EVOS. The problem is repeated, not one matchup.
- 1076 was a 3–13 and 2–20 kill loss to GEEK, the low point of the season; 1062 was a one-sided loss to DEWA.
- First selection is not the same as a side advantage; the database does not record draft order well enough to separate the two.

**Coaching read.** Red-side draft is the highest-leverage fix in the league. Opponents should force ONIC to red and prepare for the Samuel-mid experiment (1069) and the early turtle trap. ONIC's own prep should treat first selection as a resource, not a comfort.
