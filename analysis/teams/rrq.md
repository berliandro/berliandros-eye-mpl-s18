## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 4–12 series, 14–27 games; 1 sweep won and 6 lost; 9 deciders. Average game 16:47. Blue side 10–14, red 4–13. Close games (under 3,000 gold) 2–1; blowouts (10,000+) 5–11. Source: `Q-ALL-TEAM-SEASON`, `Q-ALL-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-TEAM-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-PICKS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Supported reading.**

- RRQ played the most deciders in the league (9) and won only 4 series. They are not getting blown out every week; they are losing close series. That is a different coaching problem from DEWA's collapse.
- The draft has the clearest volume-result mismatch in the league: Paquito 17 picks at 23.5%, Hirara 10 at 30%, Uranus 7 at 28.6%, Brody 6 at 16.7%. Rafaela (11, 45.5%) and Aulus (8, 50%) are the steadier pieces.
- 1039 is the positive template: RRQ survive an early DEWA push, take the Lord, and win 10–1 in kills (`TOBS-1039-G1-005`, `-012`, `-022`). 1100 shows the same shape in a game 1 win over NAVI, with a first Turtle and a 828-second finish (`TOBS-1100-G1-07`, `-13`).
- The failure mode is repeated: 1065 loses both Turtles and the middle outer turret; 1087 takes the Turtle but loses four members; 1098 falls 2,000 gold behind in four minutes and gives up four kills into a Lord (`TOBS-1065-G1-006` to `-009`, `TOBS-1087-G1-006`, `TOBS-1098-G1-007`, `-010`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- RRQ beat DEWA, GEEK twice and EVOS, so the plan works against specific opponents. The 9 deciders mean the gap is execution, not access.
- Paquito's 23.5% is descriptive; it includes different players and matchups.
- Red side 4–13 is as bad as ONIC's, but RRQ do not have ONIC's blue-side ceiling.

**Coaching read.** Stop treating Paquito as a comfort pick; the evidence says the volume is losing. Force RRQ into a front-to-back fight and make the first Turtle decisive. RRQ's own prep should focus on closing deciders — they reach them more than anyone else.
