# TLID — TEAM LIQUID ID transcript notes

Team page for TEAM LIQUID ID (TLID).

## Evidence-backed profile

**Season record.** 12–4 series, 26–11 games; 9 sweeps won and 2 lost; 5 deciders. Average game 15:14. Blue side 15–3, red 11–8. Close games (under 3,000 gold) 1–0; blowouts (10,000+) 12–4. Source: `Q-ALL-TEAM-SEASON`, `Q-ALL-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-TEAM-GAMES`, `Q-ALL-PICKS`, snapshot `mpl-s18-db-20c5ea2c51f3`.

**Supported reading.**

- TLID convert leads into short games better than anyone: 12 blowout wins, 9 sweeps, and a 15:14 average. Only one game all season was decided by under 3,000 gold, so "clutch" is not their identity; "finish" is.
- The draft is deep and efficient: Claude (16, 68.8%), Paquito (14, 71.4%), Hirara (9, 88.9%), Zhuxin (8, 87.5%), Belerick (9, 77.8%), Carmilla (8, 75%). The high win rates on contested picks are descriptive, but the volume is real.
- 1107 describes the mechanism: fast Rapid Boots rotations contest the healing Turtle, a three-player zone locks ONIC's five in the top area, and the third Turtle opens the mid base turret (`TOBS-1107-G1-02`, `-05`, `-06`, `-07`). The caster calls game one total domination (`TOBS-1107-G1-14`).
- 1080 shows the same plan against BTR: TLID force Moreno out, take Turtle and purple buff, then convert Turtle into Lord (`TOBS-1080-G1-006`, `-009`).

**Counterexamples and limits.**

- The four losses are real: 0–2 to NAVI (1044), 1–2 to EVOS (1083), 1–2 to DEWA (1085), 0–2 to NAVI (1093). NAVI beat them twice, including a sweep, so the structure is breakable.
- Blue 15–3 is not proof of a side advantage; side is confounded with draft order and opponent. The red record (11–8) is merely good.
- The close-game sample is one game, so there is no evidence about their late-game decision-making under pressure.

**Coaching read.** The counter-plan is to deny the first Turtle rotation and make TLID play a low-economy, front-to-back game. Test whether their three-player zone survives when the first Turtle is contested. Do not copy the 1107 draft as a rule; copy the rotation timing.
