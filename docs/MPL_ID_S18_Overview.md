## Format (from wikitext RSFormat + PLFormat)
- Regular Season: Double Round Robin, All Bo3, 1 pt per match win / 0 per loss. Top 6 advance to Playoffs. Tiebreakers: 1. Matches Win, 2. Diff, 3. H2H.
- Playoffs: Hybrid elimination
  - Seeds 3-6 compete Round 1 (Single elim)
  - Seeds 1-2 get bye to Round 2 (Double elim)
  - All matches Bo5 except Lower Bracket Final & Grand Final Bo7
  - Champion + Runner-up qualify to M8 World Championship
