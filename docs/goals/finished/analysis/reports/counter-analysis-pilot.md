# Counter-analysis (antithesis) — pilot semantic insights

This document challenges the three pilot insights and records what would weaken or overturn them. It is an audit-style counterweight, not a replacement.

## INS-1036-EARLY-DRAFT (`cross-checked`)

**Thesis:** the caster's early-snowball framing is consistent with EVOS's Paquito/Eudora/Melissa game-1 lineup and a 16:51 tower-heavy win.

**Antithesis:**
- Consistency is not causation. EVOS won game 2 of the same series in 24:40 with the same Paquito/Eudora core but a different gold lane (Clint instead of Melissa), so the Melissa-specific "turret hitter" claim is not tested by game 2.
- The counterexample set (1055 g2, 1066 g2, 1077 g1, 1103 g1) shows the core loses; but those losses may share a different opposing draft or macro, so the counterexamples do not isolate the draft as the cause either.
- Tower damage (22,472 vs 1,252) is an outcome aggregate. A fast win against a collapsed opponent inflates it; it does not measure intentional early conversion.
- Auto-captions may have mis-heard hero names; the cross-check is against the database lineup, not audio.

**What would be needed to upgrade:** matched comparisons across EVOS wins/losses with the same core against similar opposing drafts, plus first-turret/first-Turtle timings. Until then the insight stays "consistent with", not "caused by".

## INS-1037-LATE-SWING (`partially-supported`)

**Thesis:** AE's game-1 win was a late defensive swing rather than wire-to-wire map control.

**Antithesis:**
- The reviewed frame at 21:08 shows AE defending base with a ~1.9k gold deficit, but a single still frame cannot show whether AE was already winning the fight or about to lose; the "clutch 1v3" is caster framing of a replay, not an observed sequence.
- AE's final +2,031 gold with less tower damage (20,158 vs 33,132) is consistent with a comeback, but it is also consistent with NAVI taking towers early and then losing a late fight; both stories fit the same numbers.
- Close-gold winners with less tower damage also occur in 1074 g2 and 1078 g2, so the pattern is not AE-specific.
- The frame at 04:15:45 failed and was not used; the fight sequence between 04:13:51 and 04:14:06 is not fully captured.

**What would be needed to upgrade:** a full clip from the first NAVI threat through the Lord attempt, plus a comparison with another close AE game. The verdict remains `partially supported`.

## INS-1107-EARLY-CONVERSION (`partially-supported`)

**Thesis:** TLID's game-1 win was fast and structure-heavy, consistent with early rotations and Rapid Boots.

**Antithesis:**
- Tower damage (32,659 vs 1,902) is an outcome of a 14:47 stomp; it does not show that Rapid Boots rotations caused the structures.
- The "40 LORD KILL" overlay is a season milestone, not a current objective, and could be misread as evidence of a Lord take.
- The 07:06:20 frame was a YouTube Premium promo and was correctly rejected, but that rejection also removes one potential check on the Turtle sequence.
- TLID also lost games with substantial tower damage (1044 g1-g2, 1083 g2, 1093 g2) and won with lower tower damage (1095 g2), so tower damage is not a signature.
- The caster's "Aran" reference and "Drichel" overlay name are not joined to database handles, so no player-level claim is supportable.

**What would be needed to upgrade:** first-outer-turret and Turtle timings for 1107 g1 versus a TLID loss with a comparable early gold lead. The verdict remains `partially supported`.

## Shared antithesis (all three)

- All three insights rest on a single game each; none has matched comparisons or patch partitioning.
- Auto-captions have ASR errors and no speaker labels; quotes are verified against text, not audio.
- The database has no objective counts, gold timelines, communications or vision, so causal claims are unsupported by construction.
- "Cross-checked" and "partially-supported" are verification states about evidence quality, not proof of a team trait.
